ABOUT THE PROJECT

Words tell us how language grows.

The New Word Lab is a small student project about English vocabulary and word formation.

Our purpose

This mini dictionary makes word formation easier to notice. Each entry connects a word's meaning with the parts and process that created it, then shows the word in a natural sentence.

Why these words?

The three entries demonstrate distinct patterns: frenemy is a familiar blend, screenfog is a clearly labeled project coinage, and cyberbullying illustrates prefixation. Together, they give learners examples of combining word forms and adding an affix.

01

Blending · Frenemy

Parts of friend and enemy are blended into one portmanteau word.

02

Compounding · Screenfog

The free morphemes screen and fog combine to form a compound noun.

03

Affixation · Cyberbullying

The prefix cyber- is attached to bullying; this is prefixation.
