THE MINI DICTIONARY

Three words to explore

Read the meaning, notice how each word is formed, and see it in context.

01noun

Frenemy

A person who is both a friend and a rival; a person who is both an ally and an opponent.

Word formation

Blending (portmanteau): friend + enemy. Parts of both words are combined to form one word.

Example Although they trained together, each runner knew her frenemy wanted to win.

02noun · coined for this project

Screenfog

A hazy look on a screen caused by glare, smudges, or prolonged viewing; figuratively, a foggy or sluggish feeling after looking at screens for a long time.

Word formation

Compounding: screen + fog. Two free morphemes combine to form a compound noun.

Example After hours of reading, I took a break when screenfog made the page hard to see.

03noun

Cyberbullying

The act of bullying another person through the internet or electronic devices.

Word formation

Affixation (prefixation): the prefix cyber- is added to bullying to create a noun for bullying carried out through digital technology.

Example The school teaches students how to report cyberbullying and support one another online.
